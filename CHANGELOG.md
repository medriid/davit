# Changelog

Downloads are on the [releases page](https://github.com/medriid/davit/releases). daVIT updates itself after the first install.

## 0.18.0 — 2026-11-03

- **Read PDFs in the app**: View opens course materials, circulars and more right here, without filling your downloads. Save keeps a copy.
- **Teachers a tap away**: tap the faculty on a course to jump to their details.
- **Faster syncs**: daVIT skips what rarely changes, and pulling down still reads everything.
- **Fixes**: circulars now download, Rules and FAQ no longer hang, and the app no longer risks crashing when returning to a screen.

## 0.17.3 — 2026-11-03

- **App lock works**: turning it on used to crash daVIT every time it opened. If that happened to you, install this over the top and your data is still there.
- **Fewer false alerts**: moving between semesters no longer announces all your marks as new.
- **Labs are square** on Home's path and theory classes round, with a little more room under the path. It shows the time, so the details below no longer repeat it.

## 0.17.2 — 2026-11-03

- **Stays on the current semester**: registering for next semester no longer moves daVIT to it. It moves once that semester's first class has been held.
- **A shorter Home**: the date, today's classes and your next class share one panel, so the quick buttons show without scrolling.

## 0.17.1 — 2026-11-02

- **Cleaner marks**: spreadsheet formulas that reach VTOP's remarks, like SUM(G48:K48), no longer show. Your marks are unchanged.
- **A tidier Home**: once today's classes are over, the last class doesn't get a second card. Tap it on the path to see it.

## 0.17.0 — 2026-11-02

- **Circulars**: every official circular in one searchable list, with an alert when a new one lands.
- **What's open**: daVIT checks registrations like transport, MOOC, internship and hostel booking once a day and tells you when one opens.
- **Faculty projects**: research open to students, searchable by area, with teacher ratings and alerts for the areas you follow.
- **More from VTOP**: rules and FAQ, make-up and other exams, certificates, sports and gym seats, all clubs, and your sign-in activity.
- **Faster refreshes**: assignments are only re-read when something has changed.

## 0.16.0 — 2026-11-01

- **Faculty reviews**: student ratings and reviews from No8AM for 3,116 teachers at Vellore and Chennai. Search by name, department or course. They're built into the app, so they work offline, and they stop at 1 November 2026.
- **Ratings on teacher cards**: teachers on VTOP's search and your courses show their rating when they can be matched, and a tap opens their reviews.
- **Shorter credits** in Settings, with one for No8AM.

## 0.15.1 — 2026-11-01

- **FFCS result, redone**: a real timetable grid with a colour for each course, and a table of your courses with slot, room and credits.

## 0.15.0 — 2026-11-01

- **FFCS result**: see the classes you registered for next semester, with your week and any clashes, as soon as VTOP has them. Find it next to FFCS.
- **Check for updates** is now a button at the end of Settings, and daVIT looks for new versions every three hours instead of once a day.

## 0.14.1 — 2026-11-01

- **A cleaner display card on Home**: no overall chip or More button, since both are right below it.

## 0.14.0 — 2026-11-01

- **Mess menu**: November's Special Mess for the men's hostel, day by day, with meal reminders. Times are borrowed from the Non-Veg Mess for now, and 10-14 and 24-28 November aren't on the sheet yet.
- **Search materials** has a filter for subject code, faculty and module.
- **Your course names** now show on the screens that still used VTOP's.
- **A shorter Home in Classic**: quick buttons near the top and fewer long lists.
- **Overall attendance** on Classic's Home now matches the top bar.

## 0.13.1 — 2026-10-31

- **Overall attendance** is the average of your subjects' percentages, so a subject with many classes no longer outweighs the rest.

## 0.13.0 — 2026-10-31

- **Settings, sorted**: six groups on one screen, each showing how it's set, each with its own short page, and **Find a setting** to jump straight to anything.
- **The daVIT logo takes you Home** from any page.

## 0.12.0 — 2026-10-31

- **FFCS** (was Timetable planner) has a smart mode: pick your subjects, the faculty you'd take and how you like your days (8:00 starts, start and end times, short lunches, days off, gaps), and get the best clash-free timetables.
- **Saved plans**: keep any timetable under a name and open it again later.
- **The campus map ships with the app**, so it works on a first open even when the map site is down.

## 0.11.1 — 2026-10-31

- **Plan this semester** can leave a course out while its grade isn't known, and shows each course's code and theory or lab.
- **Pearl Research Park** is spelled right on the map.

## 0.11.0 — 2026-10-31

- **Exam board**: every paper's time, room, seat and countdown, the free days between, and the course's materials a tap away.
- **Attendance corrections**: mark days VTOP has you absent by mistake, send the list to your faculty, and get told when they're fixed.
- **Free time with friends**: swap weeks by QR code, phone to phone, and see when you're all out of class.
- **Timetable planner** for next semester, from VTOP's course allocation, with clashes shown as you pick.

## 0.10.1 — 2026-10-31

- **Assignment uploads work**: files go to VTOP the way its own page sends them, with real progress. If VTOP signed you out meanwhile, daVIT signs in again instead of failing.
- **Labs count sessions**: a lab that met 28 times shows 28, not VTOP's 56 (the percentage is unchanged).

## 0.10.0 — 2026-10-31

- **Two styles**: Classic, the printed cards in your colours, or the new **Editorial**, a Swiss grid of big numbers and live tiles on cream (near-black at night). Pick one when you first open daVIT, or in Settings → Look.
- **Editorial's Home** has every page sorted into Study, Time, Campus, Requests & records, and Yours.
- **Your own course names**: call a course whatever you call it, and that name shows everywhere, widgets and alerts included.

## 0.9.0 — 2026-10-30

- **Follow a route**: press Start and the map stays on you, turned the way you're going, with the next turn, what's left, and a new route if you stray.
- **Monochrome glass**: a new look in black, white and greys with frosted glass cards, by day and night.
- **Home** (was Today) keeps the display card; every other tab has a plain bar.

## 0.8.1 — 2026-10-30

- **The top bar is the display card**: your background, stats and mascot in one slim card, instead of a second big card on Today. Your name can replace "daVIT" in it.

## 0.8.0 — 2026-10-30

- **Light mode in pastel**: soft card colours with black outlines and hard shadows, buttons in your accent colour, and a new Lime + Ink pair.
- **A display card on Today**: a built-in print or your own photo, two stats of your choice in its corners, your mascot and your name. Tap it to change it.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-10-30

- **Calmer colours**: night print on warm charcoal with toned-down inks (no more glow), softer cards by day.
- **Scan to PDF uploads straight from the scanner**, with one confirm, and compresses big scans to fit VTOP's 4 MB.
- **Welcome before sign-in**: pick light or dark, colours and your mascot. A short setup follows the first sync.
- **Plan a leave**: pick the days you'll be away and see every course after, day orders and holidays included.
- **Classes in your phone's calendar**, kept up to date, and an **attendance widget**. More and Settings sit in the top bar.

## 0.6.1 — 2026-10-30

- **Week** follows the academic calendar: a Saturday on Wednesday's order shows Wednesday's classes (it said "No classes"), holidays and exam days show none, and each day says why.
- Your teachers, the calendar and the current semester now come only from VTOP.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-30

- **Updates by itself**: a new release downloads in the background and installs on your tap.
- **Requests from your phone**, each shown back before it's sent: night slip, mess complaint, bonafide, paper seeing and re-evaluation, course feedback, club event registration.
- **More from VTOP**: any teacher by name, your mess, co/extra-curricular record, QCM and course allocation, event certificates.
- **Colours**: each kind of card in its own ink, and your own colours for all of them.
- **Today** as a path through the day's classes; the **map** reads better and routes from where you are.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-28

First public build: attendance by risk, Today, Academics, Week, background sync and alerts, and a home-screen widget.

# Changelog

Downloads are on the [releases page](https://github.com/medriid/davit/releases). daVIT updates itself after the first install.

## 0.11.0 — 2026-10-31

- **Exam board**: every paper's time, room, seat and countdown, the free days between, and the course's materials a tap away.
- **Attendance corrections**: mark days VTOP has you absent by mistake, send the list to your faculty, and get told when they're fixed.
- **Free time with friends**: swap weeks by QR code, phone to phone, and see when you're all out of class.
- **Timetable planner** for next semester, from VTOP's course allocation, with clashes shown as you pick.

## 0.10.1 — 2026-10-31

- **Assignment uploads work**: files go to VTOP the way its own page sends them, with real progress. If VTOP signed you out meanwhile, daVIT signs in again instead of failing.
- **Labs count sessions**: a lab that met 28 times shows 28, not VTOP's 56 (the percentage is unchanged).

## 0.10.0 — 2026-10-31

- **Two styles**: Classic, the printed cards in your colours, or the new **Editorial**, a Swiss grid of big numbers and live tiles on cream (near-black at night). Pick one when you first open daVIT, or in Settings → Look.
- **Editorial's Home** has every page sorted into Study, Time, Campus, Requests & records, and Yours.
- **Your own course names**: call a course whatever you call it, and that name shows everywhere, widgets and alerts included.

## 0.9.0 — 2026-10-30

- **Follow a route**: press Start and the map stays on you, turned the way you're going, with the next turn, what's left, and a new route if you stray.
- **Monochrome glass**: a new look in black, white and greys with frosted glass cards, by day and night.
- **Home** (was Today) keeps the display card; every other tab has a plain bar.

## 0.8.1 — 2026-10-30

- **The top bar is the display card**: your background, stats and mascot in one slim card, instead of a second big card on Today. Your name can replace "daVIT" in it.

## 0.8.0 — 2026-10-30

- **Light mode in pastel**: soft card colours with black outlines and hard shadows, buttons in your accent colour, and a new Lime + Ink pair.
- **A display card on Today**: a built-in print or your own photo, two stats of your choice in its corners, your mascot and your name. Tap it to change it.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-10-30

- **Calmer colours**: night print on warm charcoal with toned-down inks (no more glow), softer cards by day.
- **Scan to PDF uploads straight from the scanner**, with one confirm, and compresses big scans to fit VTOP's 4 MB.
- **Welcome before sign-in**: pick light or dark, colours and your mascot. A short setup follows the first sync.
- **Plan a leave**: pick the days you'll be away and see every course after, day orders and holidays included.
- **Classes in your phone's calendar**, kept up to date, and an **attendance widget**. More and Settings sit in the top bar.

## 0.6.1 — 2026-10-30

- **Week** follows the academic calendar: a Saturday on Wednesday's order shows Wednesday's classes (it said "No classes"), holidays and exam days show none, and each day says why.
- Your teachers, the calendar and the current semester now come only from VTOP.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-30

- **Updates by itself**: a new release downloads in the background and installs on your tap.
- **Requests from your phone**, each shown back before it's sent: night slip, mess complaint, bonafide, paper seeing and re-evaluation, course feedback, club event registration.
- **More from VTOP**: any teacher by name, your mess, co/extra-curricular record, QCM and course allocation, event certificates.
- **Colours**: each kind of card in its own ink, and your own colours for all of them.
- **Today** as a path through the day's classes; the **map** reads better and routes from where you are.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-28

First public build: attendance by risk, Today, Academics, Week, background sync and alerts, and a home-screen widget.
